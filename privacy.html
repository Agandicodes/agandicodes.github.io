1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all AgandiCodes products and services, including but not limited to:

  • Mobile and web applications (e.g., Luganda Bible, educational tools)
  • Games (casual, educational, and interactive experiences)
  • IoT (Internet of Things) software and device integrations
  • HSE (Health, Safety, and Environment) monitoring and compliance tools
  • Developer utilities, APIs, and this website

By using any of these Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on which Service you use:

  • Device & Usage Data: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, crash logs, and general usage statistics.
  • Account Data: if you create an account, we may collect your email address and display name.
  • Game Data: in-game progress, virtual item inventory, and gameplay analytics.
  • IoT & HSE Data: sensor readings, device status, and environmental metrics collected by your IoT/HSE devices (only with your consent and configured settings).
  • Communications: messages you send through our contact forms or chatbot.

Personally identifiable information (PII) is only collected with your explicit consent or as required to provide a Service.

3. How We Use Data

We use collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Services
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., game recommendations, app settings)
  • Display relevant ads (with your consent where required)
  • Analyze usage trends and fix bugs
  • Ensure the safety and security of our IoT and HSE tools
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, we process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: for personalized ads, optional analytics, and IoT data collection.
  • Contract: to provide Services you have requested.
  • Legitimate Interest: for security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.
  • Legal Obligation: to comply with applicable laws.

5. Third-Party Services

We may integrate third-party tools and SDKs that have their own privacy practices, including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (analytics, crash reporting)
  • AdMob (advertising)
  • Cloud hosting and data storage providers

We encourage you to review their privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

6. Games, IoT & HSE Data

Games: We collect gameplay analytics to improve game balance and user experience. In-app purchases are processed by platform providers (Google Play, Apple App Store) — we do not store payment card details.

IoT & HSE: Sensor data from IoT/HSE devices is collected only if you enable data sharing. This data is used to provide monitoring, alerts, and compliance reports. You can disable data collection at any time in your device settings. We do not share raw IoT data with third parties without your explicit consent.

7. International Compliance

We are committed to complying with global privacy regulations, including:

  • GDPR (EU/EEA): including data subject rights and lawful processing.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): including the right to know, delete, and opt-out of the sale of personal information.
  • COPPA (US): protecting children's privacy online.
  • PIPEDA (Canada) and other applicable local laws.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU/EEA where applicable) without verifiable parental consent. For games and HSE tools intended for younger users, we implement age-gating and parental consent mechanisms. If we become aware that a child has submitted personal information without consent, we will delete it immediately.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at agandicodes@gmail.com.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your data, including encryption of sensitive transmissions and access controls. No system is 100% secure, but we continuously review and improve our safeguards.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the Services or via email. The "last updated" date will be revised accordingly. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this policy or your data rights, contact us at: agandicodes@gmail.com
